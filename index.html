About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Ju Ren. I received my BSc in Computer Science and Technology from Xuteli School, Beijing Institute of Technology, in 2024.

My research interests include self-improvement, agentic reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and deep reinforcement learning.
